About us

Faith, mission, and service across Western Uganda.

Western Uganda Field is a regional administrative unit of the Seventh-day Adventist Church committed to worship, discipleship, church growth, and compassionate outreach across seven districts.

Our Call

To make disciples of Jesus Christ who live as loving witnesses, proclaim the everlasting gospel, and embody the mercy and mission of the church in every community.

Field profile

Serving the people of Western Uganda

Our work is centered on spiritual nurture, practical service, and partnerships that strengthen local churches and their communities.

Mission

To preach the gospel, disciple believers, and equip members for service throughout the districts under the care of Western Uganda Field.

Vision

Healthy worshiping communities rooted in Christ, marked by prayer, integrity, evangelism, and compassionate outreach.

Coverage

From the headquarters at Kikwite, Hoima, we serve communities in Buliisa, Hoima, Kagadi, Kakumiro, Kibaale, Kiryandongo, and Masindi.

Who we are

Western Uganda Field is part of the Seventh-day Adventist Church in Uganda, organized under the Uganda Union Mission and connected to the broader East-Central Africa Division and world church.

What we do

  • Pastoral oversight and support for churches
  • Departmental leadership and ministry coordination
  • Education, health, and community outreach
  • Evangelism and church growth initiatives
Core values

Grounded in Christ and service

Worship

We honor God through faithful worship, spiritual nurture, and a reverence for His Word and commandments.

Discipleship

We help members grow in Christ, be rooted in Scripture, and live as witnesses of His love.

Community service

We extend compassion through schools, health initiatives, social care, and service to families and neighbors.
