About the Field

Serving Western Uganda Through Faith and Mission

Western Uganda Field is an administrative unit of the Seventh-day Adventist Church under the Uganda Union Mission, within the East-Central Africa Division. From its headquarters at Kikwite, along Kampala Road in Hoima, the Field coordinates the work of the Church across seven districts of Western Uganda.

The Field supports pastors, local church leaders and members as they worship God, make disciples, nurture believers and serve their communities through education, health ministry, literature evangelism and compassionate outreach.

Who We Are

Serving Western Uganda Through Faith and Mission

Western Uganda Field is an administrative unit of the Seventh-day Adventist Church, operating under the Uganda Union Mission within the East-Central Africa Division. Its headquarters is located at Kikwite, Hoima, along Kampala Road.

As a Field, we provide leadership, pastoral support, departmental ministry and administrative services to local churches and congregations within our territory. Our purpose is to equip members for worship, discipleship, evangelism and community service.

The Field's territory comprises the districts of Buliisa, Hoima, Kagadi, Kakumiro, Kibaale, Kiryandongo and Masindi — a diverse region of lakeshore communities, agricultural towns, growing urban centres and rural congregations.

Our Educational Mission

Educating for Eternity

The Education Department of Western Uganda Field supports Adventist schools and educational programmes within the Field's territory. Adventist education seeks to develop the whole person — intellectually, physically, socially and spiritually — in preparation for service in this world and the world to come.

The department works with school administrators, teachers and chaplains to promote academic excellence, character development and a Christ-centred learning environment.

Our Health Mission

Healing Ministry in the Community

Health Ministries in Western Uganda Field promotes the Adventist health message and encourages healthy living through education, prevention and compassionate service. The ministry supports local churches in serving their communities through health programmes and community outreach.

Health ministry is part of the Church's broader mission of service — caring for the body as well as the spirit, following the example of Christ who healed the sick and ministered to those in need.

Home/Our Story

Our Story

The history of Adventist work in Western Uganda and the development of Western Uganda Field.

Historical content pending. The Field is compiling its official history. No historical dates, milestones or events have been invented here. The timeline below uses clearly marked placeholder entries that will be replaced with verified information.

Western Uganda Field is part of the Seventh-day Adventist Church's organised work in Uganda under the Uganda Union Mission. The Church's presence in the region has grown over the years into a network of congregations, schools and community ministries serving the people of Buliisa, Hoima, Kagadi, Kakumiro, Kibaale, Kiryandongo and Masindi.

Today the Field coordinates the spiritual and administrative life of 131 churches and more than 32,000 members, supporting pastors, local church leaders and departmental ministries as they serve their communities.
